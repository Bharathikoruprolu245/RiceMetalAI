# RiceMetalAI

## AI-Assisted Structural Bioinformatics Platform for Heavy Metal Transport Proteins in Rice

### Vision

RiceMetalAI is an integrated bioinformatics platform that combines:

- Gene annotation
- Protein characterization
- Structural biology
- Molecular docking
- AI analysis
- CRISPR guide design

to discover optimal genome editing strategies for reducing heavy metal accumulation in rice.
